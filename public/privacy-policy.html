Plusveda — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Plusveda is pharmacy inventory, billing and GST software for medical stores. It is a business tool, sold to and used by pharmacy staff. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to get it deleted.

The app is published by FiveM Infotech. For anything in this policy, contact 5fivempvt@gmail.com.

1. Who the data belongs to

Two different kinds of people appear in Plusveda, and they are treated differently:

  • Pharmacy staff — the people who sign in. We hold their account details in order to run the service.
  • The pharmacy's own customers — people the pharmacy chooses to record against a bill. We store this on the pharmacy's behalf. The pharmacy decides what to enter, and the pharmacy is responsible for having the right to enter it. We do not use it for anything of our own.

2. What is collected

Data Why
Name, email address, mobile number, password (stored hashed) To create and secure a staff account
Pharmacy details — shop name, address, GSTIN, drug licence number, and the signature or stamp image if uploaded To print legally compliant GST invoices
Business records — products, batches, expiry dates, stock levels, suppliers, purchase bills, sales invoices, payments The core purpose of the app
Customer name and mobile number, where the pharmacy chooses to record them against a sale So the pharmacy can reprint a bill, process a return, or set a refill reminder. Optional — sales can be recorded as walk-in
Photographs taken in the app — distributor bills, medicine packs, cheques To read printed text so it does not have to be typed (see §4)
Sign-in session records — device type, IP address, time of sign-in To enforce the limit on how many devices one account may be signed in on, and to let a user sign out a device they no longer have
Activity log — which user performed which action, and when Audit trail. A pharmacy needs to know who changed stock or cancelled a bill
Plusveda does not collect health records. It records what a pharmacy sold, not any diagnosis, prescription history or medical condition of a named person.

3. Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — to scan barcodes on medicine packs, and to photograph distributor bills, pack panels and cheques. Images are used only for that purpose. The camera is never used in the background.
  • Internet — the app is a cloud service; all data is stored on our servers rather than only on the device.

4. Photographs and automated reading

When a bill, pack or cheque is photographed, the image is sent to Google's Gemini API (Google LLC) to read the printed text — product names, batch numbers, expiry dates, quantities and amounts. This is what removes the typing.

This means the image leaves our servers and is processed by Google. Google processes it as our service provider under the Gemini API terms, and does not use it to train its models. We do not send account passwords, customer names or sales records for this purpose — only the photograph itself.

Cheque images are stored so the pharmacy can refer back to them. Distributor bill and pack photographs are used to extract the text and are not retained afterwards.

5. Who else the data is shared with

Plusveda does not sell data, does not share it for advertising, and contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. Data is handled by the following service providers, solely to run the service:

Provider What it handles
MongoDB Atlas The database — all business and account records
Render The application server
Vercel The web version of the app
Google (Gemini API) Reading text from photographed bills, packs and cheques (§4)
Cloudinary Storage of uploaded images such as cheque photographs
Expo (EAS) Delivery of app updates
Email provider (SMTP) Transactional email only — password reset, account invitations, and expiry alerts where the pharmacy has turned them on

We may also disclose data where the law requires it, or to establish or defend a legal claim.

6. Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored hashed with bcrypt; we cannot read them.
  • Access within a pharmacy is permission-based — an owner decides what each member of staff can see and do.
  • Each account is limited to a set number of signed-in devices, and any device can be signed out remotely from the profile screen.
  • Every pharmacy's data is separated from every other pharmacy's at the database level.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you.

7. How long data is kept

Business records are kept for as long as the pharmacy's account is active, because invoices and stock history have to be retained for tax and drug licensing purposes. Indian GST rules generally require records to be kept for six years, and we retain them accordingly unless deletion is requested.

Sign-in session records are kept until the session is ended or expires. Distributor bill and pack photographs are not retained after the text has been read.

8. Deleting your account and data

You can request deletion of your account and its data at any time:

  • In the app: Profile → Delete account, where available, or
  • By emailing 5fivempvt@gmail.com from the address on the account.

We will confirm the request and delete the account and its business records within 30 days, other than anything we are legally required to keep (for example, tax records still within their statutory retention period). Where that applies, the retained data is not used for any other purpose and is deleted once the period ends.

A full description is at /delete-account.html.

9. Children

Plusveda is business software for licensed pharmacies. It is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it. Write to 5fivempvt@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and notify account holders by email. Continuing to use Plusveda after a change means you accept the updated policy.
