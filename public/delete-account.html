Delete your Plusveda account

Last updated: 8 August 2026

This page explains how to have your Plusveda account and its data deleted, what gets removed, and what we are obliged to keep.

How to request deletion

  1. Email 5fivempvt@gmail.com from the email address on the account, with the subject "Delete my Plusveda account".
  2. Include your pharmacy name so we can identify the workspace.
  3. We reply to confirm — deletion is irreversible, so we check before acting.
  4. The account and its data are deleted within 30 days.

Request account deletion

If you are a staff member rather than the pharmacy owner, we will confirm with the owner before deleting a shared workspace — one employee cannot erase a pharmacy's records.

Your login, name, email, mobile number and password

Sign-in sessions and activity logs 		Records we are legally required to retain — chiefly GST invoice data, which Indian tax law generally requires to be kept for six years. Anything retained on that basis is not used for any other purpose, is not linked to a live account, and is deleted once the statutory period ends.

How long it takes

We acknowledge within 3 working days and complete deletion within 30 days of confirming your request. Backups are rotated on a 30-day cycle, so data may persist in an encrypted backup for up to 30 days after deletion before being overwritten.

Deleting one staff member instead

If you only want to remove one person's access rather than the whole pharmacy, the owner can do that in the app under Team & Access without contacting us. Their sales and audit history stays on the pharmacy's records, which is what an audit trail is for.
